The reader opening a technical reference is rarely sitting in a quiet armchair with unlimited patience. More often, a deployment is stalled, a service is timing out, or they are switching between three terminal windows trying to grasp an unfamiliar abstraction.

### Respecting Cognitive Bandwidth

Great technical prose treats the reader's attention as scarce:

- **Lead with the decision or symptom**: Tell the reader what a pattern prevents before explaining its history.
- **Anchor every pronoun**: Replace vague phrases like "This handles the edge case" with exact nouns such as "The bounded retry loop handles transient 503 responses."
- **Keep explanation and code adjacent**: Never describe an algorithm for four paragraphs before showing the ten lines that make it concrete.

When prose respects a tired reader, it feels effortless to an energetic one too.
