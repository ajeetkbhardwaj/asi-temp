Traditional publishing separates writing, editing, and web presentation into disconnected tools. By the time a book ships, edits made to the manuscript fall out of sync with the published reader.

### One Source of Truth

At Kumar Press, every volume lives in plain files organized by book, volume, and part:

```text
contents/
  book-1/
    vol-1/
      part-1/
        chapter-1.mdx
  articles/
    essay.mdx
```

From that single manuscript tree, the web reader renders clean typography, automatic tables of contents, and cross-chapter navigation directly on demand.
