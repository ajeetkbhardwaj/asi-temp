---
title: "Designing for Graceful Degradation"
description: "Protect core user workflows when non-critical dependencies slow down or become unavailable."
order: 2
---

A system that collapses completely when its recommendation feed or analytics pipeline slows down has failed to separate critical paths from optional enrichments.

### Separating Critical from Optional Paths

1. **Core Path**: Authentication, checkout/order commit, and primary data retrieval.
2. **Enhancement Path**: Personalization, secondary counts, and background notifications.

Use circuit breakers and cached fallbacks on enhancement paths so core functionality remains responsive even during partial infrastructure degradation.
